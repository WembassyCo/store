Most nonprofits are leaking money on redundant tools, using outdated systems, and missing critical security configurations. This 100-point checklist covers everything you need to audit your nonprofit's technology stack — from your website CMS to your CRM, donor management, email marketing, security posture, and AI readiness.

Why Audit Your Tech Stack?

  • Save money: Nonprofits waste $3,000-$15,000/year on redundant or unused software
  • Improve security: 60% of nonprofits have experienced a data breach — most preventable
  • Accessibility compliance: WCAG 2.1 AA is now legally required for nonprofits receiving federal funds
  • AI readiness: Position your organization to leverage AI tools efficiently
  • Donor experience: Frictionless donation flows increase giving by 15-30%

The 8 Audit Categories

1. Website & CMS (15 points)

  • CMS version current and supported
  • SSL certificate valid and auto-renewing
  • Mobile-responsive design verified
  • Page load speed under 3 seconds
  • WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility compliance

2. CRM & Donor Management (15 points)

  • CRM tracks all donor interactions
  • Duplicate records cleaned quarterly
  • Donation history exportable
  • Automated thank-you emails sent within 1 hour
  • Recurring donation management working

3. Email & Communications (10 points)

  • SPF, DKIM, DMARC records configured
  • Email list segmented by donor type
  • Open rate above 25%
  • A/B testing in place for campaigns

4. Online Donations (10 points)

  • Donation page loads in under 2 seconds
  • Mobile donation flow tested and optimized
  • Multiple payment methods (card, ACH, Apple/Google Pay)
  • Recurring giving option prominent

5. Security & Compliance (15 points)

  • Two-factor authentication on all admin accounts
  • Staff access reviewed quarterly
  • Data backup tested in last 90 days
  • Incident response plan documented

6. Accessibility (15 points)

  • Automated accessibility scan passed (WAVE or axe)
  • Keyboard navigation tested
  • Alt text on all images
  • Color contrast ratios meet WCAG standards

7. Integrations & Automation (10 points)

  • CRM ↔ Website sync automated
  • Donation data flows to accounting system
  • Email tool syncs with CRM automatically

8. AI Readiness (10 points)

  • Data stored in structured, queryable formats
  • Staff trained on AI tool usage policies
  • Acceptable use policy for AI tools documented
  • At least one AI tool in active use (chatbot, content, analytics)
