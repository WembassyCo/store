Website Uptime Monitoring Service

Published: November 24, 2026

Every minute your website is down, you're losing visitors, donors, customers, and trust. Most organizations don't know their site went down until a customer tells them — hours too late.

What We Monitor

  • HTTP/HTTPS status: Is your site responding at all?
  • Response time: How fast is it? (slow = effectively down)
  • SSL certificate: Expiring soon? Already expired?
  • Content checks: Does the page contain expected content?
  • DNS resolution: Is your domain resolving correctly?

How It Works

  • 60-second check intervals (Standard) or 30-second (Pro)
  • Instant alerts via email + SMS when downtime detected
  • Public status page you can share with stakeholders
  • Monthly uptime reports with SLA tracking
  • Multi-region checks (not just one server)
