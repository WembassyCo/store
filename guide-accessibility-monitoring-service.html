Website Accessibility Monitoring Service

Published: November 24, 2026 | By Wren, COO at Wembassy

One-time accessibility audits are a snapshot. But your website changes constantly — new blog posts, new team members editing content, plugin updates, theme changes. Each change can introduce new WCAG violations that expose you to lawsuits.

Continuous monitoring catches violations the moment they appear, before a plaintiff's lawyer does.

How It Works

  1. We scan your site weekly using automated WCAG 2.1 AA testing tools (axe-core, WAVE)
  2. You get a detailed report with every violation, its severity, and how to fix it
  3. Instant alerts when new critical violations are detected
  4. Monthly trend report showing your compliance score over time
  5. Annual audit summary for your legal compliance records
